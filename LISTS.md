# VALUES LISTS to be used with the custom creations.
## Entity Types (EntityType)
These are used to specify which mob to spawn.

- Hostile Mobs: ZOMBIE, SKELETON, CREEPER, SPIDER, CAVE_SPIDER, ENDERMAN, WITCH, SLIME, PHANTOM, DROWNED, HUSK, STRAY
- Nether Mobs: GHAST, BLAZE, WITHER_SKELETON, ZOMBIFIED_PIGLIN, PIGLIN, PIGLIN_BRUTE, MAGMA_CUBE
- Bosses/Heavy Hitters: ENDER_DRAGON, WITHER, WARDEN, RAVAGER, EVOKER, VINDICATOR
- Passive/Neutral Mobs: PIG, COW, SHEEP, CHICKEN, VILLAGER, IRON_GOLEM, SNOWMAN, WOLF, HORSE

Definitive Source: PaperMC Javadocs - EntityType Enum

## Item Names (Material)
These are used for specifying weapons, armor, and held items.

- Swords: WOODEN_SWORD, STONE_SWORD, IRON_SWORD, GOLDEN_SWORD, DIAMOND_SWORD, NETHERITE_SWORD
- Other Weapons: BOW, CROSSBOW, TRIDENT, MACE
- Offhand Items: SHIELD, TOTEM_OF_UNDYING, ARROW, TIPPED_ARROW
- Helmets: LEATHER_HELMET, CHAINMAIL_HELMET, IRON_HELMET, GOLDEN_HELMET, DIAMOND_HELMET, NETHERITE_HELMET, TURTLE_HELMET
- Chestplates: LEATHER_CHESTPLATE, CHAINMAIL_CHESTPLATE, IRON_CHESTPLATE, GOLDEN_CHESTPLATE, DIAMOND_CHESTPLATE, NETHERITE_CHESTPLATE
- Leggings: LEATHER_LEGGINGS, CHAINMAIL_LEGGINGS, IRON_LEGGINGS, GOLDEN_LEGGINGS, DIAMOND_LEGGINGS, NETHERITE_LEGGINGS
- Boots: LEATHER_BOOTS, CHAINMAIL_BOOTS, IRON_BOOTS, GOLDEN_BOOTS, DIAMOND_BOOTS, NETHERITE_BOOTS

Definitive Source: PaperMC Javadocs - Material Enum

## Biomes (Biome)
These are used to dictate where your custom mobs are allowed to spawn.

- Overworld (Temperate): PLAINS, SUNFLOWER_PLAINS, FOREST, FLOWER_FOREST, BIRCH_FOREST, DARK_FOREST, SWAMP, MANGROVE_SWAMP, CHERRY_GROVE
- Overworld (Warm/Hot): DESERT, SAVANNA, JUNGLE, BAMBOO_JUNGLE, BADLANDS, ERODED_BADLANDS
- Overworld (Cold): TAIGA, SNOWY_PLAINS, ICE_SPIKES, SNOWY_SLOPES, FROZEN_PEAKS, JAGGED_PEAKS
- Overworld (Underground): DEEP_DARK, DRIPSTONE_CAVES, LUSH_CAVES
- Overworld (Ocean): OCEAN, DEEP_OCEAN, WARM_OCEAN, FROZEN_OCEAN
- The Nether: NETHER_WASTES, CRIMSON_FOREST, WARPED_FOREST, SOUL_SAND_VALLEY, BASALT_DELTAS
- The End: THE_END, SMALL_END_ISLANDS, END_MIDLANDS, END_HIGHLANDS, END_BARRENS

Definitive Source: PaperMC Javadocs - Biome Enum
